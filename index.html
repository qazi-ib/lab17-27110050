FAQs

What is the format?

Ravi Open is a British Parliamentary tournament: four teams debate each motion, with two speakers per team.

How many rounds will we debate?

There are five preliminary rounds, followed by a final.

How much is registration?

Entry costs Rs 3,000 per team.

Where will the tournament take place?

The tournament will be held in Lahore. Venue details will be shared with registered teams.
