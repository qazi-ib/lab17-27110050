British Parliamentary · Ravi Open 2027

Four teams.
Two benches. One debate.

Every round puts two teams on Government against two teams on Opposition. Here is how the format works.

The room at a glance

Two benches, four teams

Each team has two speakers. Teams on the same bench support the same side, but each must make its own distinct contribution.

Government bench

Government supports the motion and explains why it should be accepted.

  • Opening Government: sets up the case.
  • Closing Government: adds a distinct extension.

Opposition bench

Opposition challenges the motion and explains why it should be rejected.

  • Opening Opposition: answers the opening case.
  • Closing Opposition: adds a distinct extension.

Eight speeches, seven minutes each

The order is Prime Minister, Leader of Opposition, Deputy Prime Minister, Deputy Leader of Opposition, Government Member, Opposition Member, Government Whip, then Opposition Whip. Each speech lasts seven minutes.

Listen, think, respond

Points of information

POIs bring direct engagement into a speech. They are brief challenges or questions from the other bench.

How to offer and take one

After the first minute and before the final minute of a speech, a speaker may offer a POI. The speaker has the choice to accept or decline it. An accepted POI should be brief, and the speaker then responds before continuing. The opening and closing minutes are protected from POIs.
